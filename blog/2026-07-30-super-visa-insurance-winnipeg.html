What is Super Visa Insurance and Why Do You Need It?

The Super Visa program is a Canadian immigration pathway that allows parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens and permanent residents to visit Canada for up to 2 years without needing to renew their visitor status. It's an incredible opportunity for families to spend quality time together—whether celebrating milestones, attending weddings, or simply enjoying each other's company.

However, there's one mandatory requirement: Super Visa insurance. This specialized medical coverage is issued by a private Canadian insurance provider and covers emergency medical care during the visitor's stay in Canada.

Why is it mandatory? The Government of Canada requires proof of private health insurance because visitors from outside Canada aren't automatically covered by provincial health plans. Without Super Visa insurance, your parent or grandparent could face catastrophic medical bills if they experience a health emergency.

Here's what you need to know: Super Visa insurance must provide a minimum of $100,000 CAD in emergency medical coverage per person. It's typically valid for one or two years, depending on your family's plans and the insurance provider you choose.

Key Features of Super Visa Insurance Coverage

When you're shopping for Super Visa insurance in Winnipeg, it's important to understand what's actually covered. Here are the essential features:

  • Emergency medical expenses: Hospital stays, doctor visits, prescription medications, and emergency dental work
  • Minimum coverage requirement: $100,000 CAD (though some families opt for higher limits for peace of mind)
  • Pre-existing condition coverage: Many policies cover pre-existing medical conditions, though this varies by provider and individual health history
  • Repatriation coverage: If your loved one becomes too ill to stay in Canada, coverage for return transportation home
  • No age limit: Super Visa insurance is available to seniors of any age, making it perfect for aging parents and grandparents
  • Flexibility: Coverage can typically be arranged for the duration of their visit, whether it's 30 days, one year, or two years

How Much Does Super Visa Insurance Cost in Winnipeg?

The cost of Super Visa insurance depends on several factors:

  • Age of the visitor (older visitors typically pay more)
  • Duration of coverage (one month vs. two years)
  • Coverage amount (minimum $100,000 or higher)
  • Pre-existing medical conditions
  • Overall health status

On average, monthly premiums for a healthy 65-year-old range from $40 to $80 CAD per month. For a 2-year policy, you might pay $600 to $1,200 annually—a small price for comprehensive peace of mind.

The good news? Most insurance providers offer flexible payment options. You can pay monthly, annually, or in lump sum, depending on what works best for your family budget.
