What is Super Visa Insurance Canada?

Super Visa insurance is mandatory health insurance coverage that you must purchase before your parents or grandparents arrive in Canada. It's specifically designed for visitors who are coming to stay for extended periods—typically up to two years per visit.

The Super Visa program itself was created by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) in December 2011. It's an alternative to the standard visitor visa and allows your parents or grandparents to stay in Canada for longer periods without having to renew their visitor status annually.

The insurance isn't optional—it's a mandatory requirement for Super Visa approval. Without proof of adequate health insurance coverage, your family members won't be able to obtain their Super Visa, no matter how strong the rest of their application is.

Super Visa Insurance Coverage: What's Included?

Super Visa insurance must provide minimum coverage of $100,000 CAD for emergency medical care. This is the baseline requirement set by Canadian immigration authorities, and your policy must meet this threshold to be approved.

Here's what's typically covered under Super Visa insurance policies:

  • Emergency hospital visits: Room and board, emergency surgical procedures, and urgent medical treatments
  • Emergency medical transportation: Ambulance services and emergency medical evacuation if needed
  • Physician services: Doctor visits and consultations for unexpected medical emergencies
  • Prescription medications: Emergency medications prescribed during their stay
  • Dental emergencies: Some policies cover emergency dental treatment for pain relief

It's important to note that Super Visa insurance covers emergency situations only—not routine or non-urgent care, pre-existing conditions (unless declared and accepted), or planned medical procedures.

Super Visa Insurance Requirements & Duration

Insurance coverage must be in place before your parents or grandparents arrive in Canada. Here are the key requirements:

  • Minimum coverage amount: $100,000 CAD for emergency medical care
  • Duration: Policy must cover the entire intended stay (up to 24 months per visit)
  • Deductible limit: Maximum deductible of $10,000 CAD per claim
  • Insurance provider: Must be from a Canadian insurance company or a foreign company approved by IRCC
  • Coverage start date: Insurance must begin on or before the date of arrival in Canada

One question I hear frequently is: "Can my parents use their home country insurance?" Generally, no. IRCC specifically requires a Canadian-recognized insurance policy. That's why it's crucial to arrange this through a licensed broker who understands the requirements.

Cost & How to Get Super Visa Insurance

The cost of Super Visa insurance varies based on several factors:

  • Age of applicants: Older visitors typically have higher premiums
  • Length of stay: Longer visits cost more than shorter ones
  • Health profile: Pre-existing conditions may increase costs or require special underwriting
  • Insurance provider: Different companies offer different rates

On average, families can expect to pay anywhere from $600 to $2,500+ CAD annually, depending on age. For a 65-year-old visiting for one year, premiums typically range from $1,500 to $2,000.
