What Exactly is Super Visa Insurance?

Super Visa insurance is a specialized health insurance policy designed specifically for parents and grandparents visiting Canada from abroad. Unlike regular travel insurance, Super Visa coverage is mandatory for anyone applying for a Super Visa to Canada—there's no way around it.

Introduced in 2011, the Super Visa program allows Canadian citizens and permanent residents to sponsor their parents and grandparents for extended visits lasting up to two years without needing a renewal. But here's the catch: visitors must have valid Super Visa insurance for the duration of their stay.

The insurance must cover:

  • Emergency medical care in Canada (minimum $200,000 CAD coverage)
  • Hospitalization
  • Doctor visits and emergency dental
  • Medical evacuation if needed
  • Repatriation of remains (in the unfortunate event of death)

Super Visa insurance isn't cheap, but it's an absolute requirement. Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) will deny any Super Visa application without proof of valid insurance.

Super Visa Insurance Requirements in Winnipeg and Canada

Whether your parents are applying from India, Pakistan, the Philippines, or any other country, the requirements are the same across Canada—including here in Winnipeg, Manitoba.

Here's what Immigration Canada requires:

  • Minimum coverage: $200,000 CAD in emergency medical care
  • Duration: Insurance must cover the entire length of the visit (up to 24 months)
  • Provider: Must be from an insurance company approved by the Canadian government
  • Timeline: Insurance must begin on or before the date of entry into Canada
  • Age consideration: Coverage must be valid even if your parent turns a year older during the visit

One common mistake families make? Thinking regular travel insurance will work. It won't. Regular travel insurance policies often exclude people over 65 or have much lower coverage limits. Super Visa insurance is specifically designed to meet government requirements.

How Much Does Super Visa Insurance Cost in Winnipeg?

The cost of Super Visa insurance varies based on several factors:

  • Age of the visitor: The older your parent, the higher the premium. A 65-year-old will pay less than an 80-year-old.
  • Length of stay: A 6-month visit costs less than a 2-year stay.
  • Pre-existing medical conditions: Health history significantly impacts premiums. Someone with diabetes or heart conditions will pay more.
  • Insurance provider: Different approved providers charge different rates.

On average, families can expect to pay between $1,500 to $4,500 CAD for a 12-month policy for a parent in their late 60s or early 70s. For longer stays or older visitors, costs can exceed $6,000.

It's a significant investment, but it protects your family from catastrophic medical costs that could easily reach hundreds of thousands of dollars.
