What is IRCC Super Visa Insurance?

The Super Visa is a temporary residence visa designed specifically for parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens and permanent residents. Unlike regular visitor visas, the Super Visa allows your loved ones to stay in Canada for up to 2 years without needing to renew their status.

However, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) requires all Super Visa applicants to have private medical insurance coverage before they can be approved. This is not optional — it's a mandatory requirement, and without it, your application will not be accepted.

Key IRCC Super Visa Insurance Requirements 2025

Minimum Coverage Amount

For 2025, the minimum private health insurance coverage required is $200,000 CAD. This amount covers emergency medical care, hospitalization, and related medical expenses during your parent's or grandparent's stay in Canada. Some provinces may have slightly different guidelines, but this federal minimum applies across Canada.

Coverage Duration

Your insurance policy must be valid for the entire duration of the Super Visa holder's stay in Canada. Since the Super Visa allows visits of up to 2 years, your insurance must cover the full period. Many families purchase annual renewable policies to ensure continuous protection throughout the visit.

Medical Examination Requirements

Before purchasing Super Visa insurance, your parent or grandparent will typically need to undergo a medical examination. IRCC-approved panel physicians conduct these exams. The medical results help insurance companies assess risk and determine premiums. These exams are straightforward and cover basic health screening, blood work, and general medical history.

Pre-existing Conditions

This is crucial for many families: Super Visa insurance policies do cover pre-existing medical conditions, which distinguishes them from regular travel insurance. However, insurers must be fully informed about any existing health conditions during the application process. Non-disclosure of pre-existing conditions can result in claim denial.

What Does Super Visa Insurance Cover?

Understanding what's included in your coverage helps ensure you've chosen the right policy. IRCC-compliant Super Visa insurance typically covers:

  • Emergency hospital stays and inpatient treatment
  • Emergency dental treatment (up to a specified limit)
  • Emergency prescription medications
  • Ambulance services
  • Diagnostic services like X-rays and lab tests
  • Physician consultations and specialist visits
  • Medical evacuation (in severe cases)

Coverage does not typically include routine check-ups, non-emergency dental work, vision care, or any treatments that could have been planned before arrival in Canada.

Cost of Super Visa Insurance 2025

Premium costs vary based on age, health status, and coverage duration. Generally, annual premiums range from $800 to $2,500 CAD for applicants aged 65 and above. Younger applicants typically pay lower premiums. Multiple-year policies often provide better value than purchasing coverage year by year.
