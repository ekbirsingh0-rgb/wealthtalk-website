What Is Super Visa Insurance and Why Do You Need It?

The Super Visa is a special entry visa program designed specifically for parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens and permanent residents. Unlike regular visitor visas, the Super Visa allows family members to stay in Canada for up to two years without renewing their status.

Here's the critical part: Canadian immigration law requires all Super Visa applicants to have private medical insurance coverage before they can even apply. This isn't optional—it's a mandatory requirement set by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).

Super Visa insurance covers medical expenses including:

  • Hospital stays and emergency care
  • Doctor visits and prescription medications
  • Dental and vision care (depending on your plan)
  • Medical evacuation to your home country if needed
  • Coverage for pre-existing medical conditions

Without proper coverage, your parents' visa application will be rejected. That's why choosing the right insurance with flexible payment options is so important for your family planning.

How Much Does Super Visa Insurance Cost Monthly?

The cost of Super Visa insurance monthly payments depends on several factors:

  • Age of applicant: Older parents (65+) typically pay more than younger family members
  • Health status: Pre-existing conditions may affect pricing
  • Duration of stay: A six-month visit costs less than a two-year Super Visa
  • Coverage level: Basic plans are more affordable than comprehensive plans with dental and vision coverage
  • Insurance provider: Different companies offer different rates and payment schedules

As a rough guide, monthly payments for a healthy parent aged 60-65 might range from $80-$150 per month, while coverage for someone over 70 could be $150-$250 monthly. The good news is that these affordable payments give your parents the protection they deserve during their Canadian visit.
