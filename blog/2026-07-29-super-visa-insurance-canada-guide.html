What is Super Visa Insurance Canada?

Super Visa insurance is a mandatory requirement for parents and grandparents visiting Canada under the Super Visa program. It's not optional—Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) will not approve your Super Visa application without proof of valid health insurance coverage.

This specialized insurance is designed specifically for temporary residents and provides coverage for emergency medical care while they're visiting you in Canada. It bridges the gap between what provincial health insurance covers and what visitors actually need during their stay.

The insurance must be purchased from a Canadian insurance company that's approved by IRCC. This isn't travel insurance you can buy from any provider—it has specific requirements and coverage minimums set by Immigration Canada.

Super Visa Insurance Requirements & Coverage Details

Immigration Canada has very specific rules about Super Visa insurance. Understanding these requirements is crucial for a successful application.

Minimum Coverage Amount

Your parent or grandparent must have insurance with a minimum coverage of $200,000 for emergency medical care. This covers hospital visits, emergency dental care (up to $200), vision care (up to $200), and prescription medications.

The coverage period must start as soon as they land in Canada and continue until they depart or their visa expires.

Deductible Limits

IRCC requires that the deductible (the amount your family member pays out-of-pocket before insurance kicks in) cannot exceed $150 per claim. Many families don't realize this—a standard travel insurance policy might have a higher deductible, which won't meet Super Visa requirements.

Pre-existing Conditions

This is where many families get stuck. Most Super Visa insurance policies have restrictions on pre-existing medical conditions. However, some approved providers offer coverage options for seniors with diabetes, high blood pressure, arthritis, or heart conditions. The premium will be higher, but it's necessary to ensure your parent is properly protected.

Duration of Coverage

Your parent can purchase insurance for anywhere from 30 days to 5 years, depending on their planned stay and age. Most families purchasing for parents aged 65+ choose annual or multi-year policies for the best rates.

Super Visa Insurance Cost: What You'll Actually Pay

One question I hear constantly: "How much does Super Visa insurance cost?"

The answer depends on several factors:

  • Age: Seniors aged 65-74 pay significantly more than younger visitors. A 70-year-old might pay $2,500-$4,000 annually, while a 50-year-old might pay $800-$1,200.
  • Health status: Pre-existing conditions increase premiums by 30-50%.
  • Coverage period: Annual policies offer better rates per month than short-term coverage.
  • Insurance provider: Different IRCC-approved providers have different pricing.

For example, if your father is 68 with controlled diabetes, expects to visit for 2 months, and needs coverage that meets IRCC requirements, you're looking at approximately $1,200-$1,500 for that period with a reputable provider.
