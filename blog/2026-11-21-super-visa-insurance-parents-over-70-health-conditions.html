Understanding Super Visa Insurance Requirements for Seniors Over 70

A Super Visa is a temporary resident visa that allows parents and grandparents to stay in Canada for up to two years without renewing. Unlike regular visitor visas, Super Visa applicants must obtain Canadian medical insurance before arriving in the country. This requirement isn't optional—it's mandatory for visa approval.

For parents over 70, insurance requirements become stricter. Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) requires that super visa insurance:

  • Provide minimum coverage of $100,000 for medical expenses
  • Cover hospital stays, doctor visits, and emergency medical care
  • Be valid for the entire duration of stay in Canada
  • Be issued by a Government of Canada-approved insurance provider
  • Have a maximum deductible of $10,000

When your parent has pre-existing health conditions, finding appropriate coverage requires working with an experienced broker who understands both insurance and immigration requirements.

The Challenge of Pre-Existing Health Conditions

Parents over 70 often manage diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, arthritis, or other chronic conditions. Many families worry that these health issues will make their parents ineligible for super visa insurance or result in prohibitively expensive premiums.

Here's the good news: having pre-existing health conditions doesn't automatically disqualify your parents from obtaining super visa insurance. However, full disclosure is crucial. When completing insurance applications, you must provide complete medical history, including:

  • Current medications and dosages
  • Recent medical test results and diagnoses
  • Hospitalizations or surgeries within the past five years
  • Ongoing treatments or specialist care
  • Lifestyle factors like smoking status

Insurance providers in Canada may impose conditions on coverage, such as specific exclusions for certain conditions or higher deductibles, but they won't deny coverage entirely simply because your parent has health issues. Finding the Right Super Visa Insurance Plan

Not all super visa insurance plans are created equal, especially for seniors over 70 with health concerns. When evaluating options, consider these factors:

  • Coverage Scope: Does the plan cover prescription medications, dental emergencies, vision care, and specialist visits?
  • Pre-Existing Condition Handling: Are your parent's specific conditions covered immediately, or is there a waiting period?
  • Premium Cost: Expect to pay between $2,000–$4,000+ annually for comprehensive coverage for seniors over 70, depending on health status
  • Repatriation Coverage: Does the plan cover emergency medical transportation back to India or your home country?
  • Direct Billing: Can healthcare providers bill the insurer directly, or will you need to pay upfront and seek reimbursement?

Many families from India, Pakistan, and other South Asian countries have found tremendous value in plans that include direct billing with Canadian hospitals and pharmacies. This eliminates language barriers and payment confusion during medical emergencies.

Steps to Secure Super Visa Insurance for Your Senior Parents

The process of obtaining super visa insurance for parents over 70 with health conditions typically involves:

  • Medical Assessment: Your parents may need to complete a questionnaire or medical examination
  • Documentation: Gather medical records, prescriptions, and doctor's letters
  • Application Submission: Submit detailed health information to the insurance provider
  • Underwriting Review: The insurer assesses risk and determines coverage terms
  • Policy Issuance: Once approved, you receive the insurance certificate required for visa application
