Visitor to Canada Insurance for Tourists and Family: Complete Guide 2026

When your loved ones visit Canada from abroad, ensuring they have proper health coverage is one of the most important decisions you can make. Visitor to Canada insurance protects your family and guests from unexpected medical emergencies that could cost thousands of dollars. Whether you're welcoming parents from India, siblings from overseas, or international friends, this guide will help you understand what coverage you need and why it matters.

What is Visitor to Canada Insurance?

Visitor to Canada insurance, also known as visitor health insurance or travel medical insurance, is a temporary health coverage plan designed specifically for people visiting Canada from other countries. Unlike Canadian provincial health plans, which only cover Canadian residents, visitor insurance bridges the gap and ensures your guests have access to emergency medical care during their stay.

This type of insurance covers emergency medical expenses including hospital stays, doctor visits, prescription medications, dental emergencies, and emergency medical evacuation if needed. For many South Asian families sponsoring elderly parents or relatives to Canada, this coverage is essential—and in some cases, mandatory for Super Visa applications.

The average cost of visitor to Canada insurance ranges from $2 to $5 per day depending on age, length of stay, and pre-existing conditions. For a 30-day visit, you might expect to pay between $60 and $150 for comprehensive coverage.

Who Needs Visitor to Canada Insurance?

Several categories of visitors should definitely have this coverage:

  • Super Visa applicants: Parents and grandparents applying for a Super Visa to visit Canadian residents must have medical insurance coverage of at least $100,000 from a Canadian insurance provider.
  • Tourists from abroad: Anyone visiting Canada on a visitor visa needs protection against medical emergencies.
  • Family members: Siblings, cousins, and other relatives visiting for extended periods should be covered.
  • International friends: Your guests from the United States, Europe, Australia, or anywhere else need visitor health insurance.
  • Temporary residents: International students and temporary workers not yet eligible for provincial health plans.

In Canada, provincial health plans don't cover visitors. If your mother visits from Punjab and gets hospitalized for pneumonia, an uninsured hospital stay could cost $20,000 or more. Emergency room visits alone can exceed $1,000 without coverage.

What Does Visitor to Canada Insurance Cover?

A comprehensive visitor insurance plan typically includes:

  • Emergency hospital and medical care
  • Emergency dental treatment (up to $500 in many plans)
  • Prescription medication expenses
  • Medical evacuation and repatriation
  • Out-of-country coverage extensions
  • Pre-existing condition coverage (with declaration)
  • 24/7 emergency support hotlines

Most plans include coverage up to $500,000 for emergency medical expenses. Some insurers offer optional add-ons for activities like skiing or adventure sports, which is helpful if your visitors plan outdoor activities across Canada.

It's important to note that visitor insurance does not typically cover routine medical care, dental cleanings, prescription glasses, or planned treatments. It's designed for emergencies only.
