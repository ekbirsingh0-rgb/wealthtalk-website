What Is Super Visa Insurance and Why Is It Required?

The Super Visa is a special immigration program designed specifically for Canadian citizens and permanent residents who want to bring their parents or grandparents to Canada for extended visits—typically up to 2 years per stay.

Unlike regular visitors, Super Visa holders must have private medical insurance before they arrive in Canada. This isn't optional—Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) makes it a mandatory requirement for approval.

Here's why this matters for your family:

  • Medical costs in Canada are expensive: A single emergency room visit can cost thousands of dollars. Without proper insurance, you'd be financially responsible for your parents' healthcare.
  • It protects your family budget: Comprehensive Super Visa insurance ensures unexpected medical expenses don't drain your savings.
  • It demonstrates financial responsibility: When you provide proof of insurance to IRCC, you're showing the government that your parents won't become a burden on Canada's healthcare system.

Think of it as peace of mind wrapped into a policy. When your parents arrive in Winnipeg, you can focus on spending quality time together instead of worrying about medical bills.

What Does Super Visa Insurance Cover in Winnipeg?

Good Super Visa insurance policies cover a range of medical needs. Here's what you should expect:

  • Emergency hospitalization: Overnight stays, surgeries, and emergency procedures
  • Prescription medications: Covered when prescribed by a Canadian doctor
  • Medical exams and tests: Diagnostic imaging, lab work, and specialist consultations
  • Dental emergencies: Some policies cover emergency dental treatment for pain or infection
  • Vision care: Emergency eye exams and corrective lens replacement
  • Ambulance services: Critical for elderly parents who may need emergency transport
  • Pre-existing conditions: Most reputable policies cover conditions your parents already have (with proper medical underwriting)

The coverage amounts typically range from $100,000 to $200,000, depending on your parents' age and health profile. For families in Winnipeg, policies usually cost between $1,500 to $3,500 per person per year.

Key Requirements for Super Visa Insurance in Canada

IRCC has specific requirements that your insurance policy must meet:

  • Minimum coverage: At least $100,000 in emergency medical coverage
  • Maximum deductible: Not more than $10,000
  • Canadian provider: The insurance must be from a Canadian insurance company or backed by one
  • Valid coverage dates: Insurance must be active on the date your parents arrive and cover their entire planned stay
  • Proof of purchase: You'll need an original policy document or certificate of insurance to submit with your Super Visa application

How to Choose the Right Super Visa Insurance for Your Family

Every family's situation is unique. Your parents' age, health history, and how long they're staying all affect which policy is best.

Consider these factors:

  • Your parents' age: Premiums increase significantly after age 65. Policies for parents in their 70s and 80s will cost more than for those in their 60s.
  • Pre-existing health conditions: Diabetes, hypertension, heart disease—these need to be disclosed. Good policies will still cover them, but premiums adjust accordingly.
  • Length of stay: Super Visa insurance is typically purchased annually, but you can get multi-year policies for families planning frequent or extended visits.
  • Deductible comfort level: Would you prefer a lower premium with a higher deductible, or vice versa?
  • Additional coverage: Some policies include trip cancellation, baggage coverage, or emergency dental—useful bonuses for your parents' journey.
