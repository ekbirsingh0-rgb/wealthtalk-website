What Exactly Is Super Visa Insurance Canada?

Super Visa insurance is a specialized health insurance product designed specifically for parents and grandparents visiting Canada under the Super Visa program. It provides coverage for emergency medical expenses during their stay in Canada—something the Canadian healthcare system won't cover for temporary visitors.

The Super Visa is an entry visa that allows your parents and grandparents to stay in Canada for up to two years without needing to renew their status. It's become incredibly popular among South Asian families in Canada, giving families extended time together without the hassle of repeated visa applications.

However, IRCC has one non-negotiable requirement: your parents must have valid private health insurance coverage for the entire duration of their stay. This is where Super Visa insurance comes in.

Super Visa Insurance Canada Requirements: What You Need to Know

The Canadian government sets specific requirements for Super Visa insurance coverage:

  • Minimum Coverage Amount: $100,000 CAD for medical expenses
  • Maximum Out-of-Pocket: No more than $150 CAD per claim (co-insurance or deductible combined)
  • Duration: Coverage must be valid for the entire period of stay in Canada
  • Pre-existing Conditions: Must be covered from day one (no waiting periods)
  • Insurer Requirements: Must be from a licensed Canadian insurance company or a recognized international insurer

These aren't suggestions—they're strict requirements. If your parents' insurance doesn't meet even one of these criteria, their Super Visa application will be denied.

How Much Does Super Visa Insurance Canada Cost?

Cost depends on several factors: your parents' age, health status, and length of stay. Generally, expect to pay between $600–$2,500 CAD annually, depending on age.

Here's a rough breakdown:

  • Ages 60-64: Approximately $600–$900/year
  • Ages 65-69: Approximately $900–$1,400/year
  • Ages 70-74: Approximately $1,400–$1,800/year
  • Ages 75+: Approximately $1,800–$2,500/year

These are estimates—actual costs vary. If your parents have pre-existing conditions like diabetes or hypertension (common among older adults), some insurers may charge slightly more, while others cover it at standard rates. This is where working with an experienced broker makes a real difference.

Choosing the Right Super Visa Insurance Policy

Not all Super Visa insurance policies are equal. Here's what to look for:

  • Comprehensive Coverage: Includes hospitalization, emergency dental, prescription medications, and emergency medical evacuation
  • No Age Limits: Some policies cut off at 80 or 85—make sure yours covers your parents' actual age
  • Flexibility: Can you extend coverage if their stay is extended? Can you adjust coverage mid-visit?
  • Customer Service: If your parents need medical help while visiting, can they speak to someone in their language (Punjabi, Hindi, or English)?
  • Fast Processing: You'll need documentation from your insurer for your visa application

I recommend getting quotes from multiple insurers before deciding. Each company has different age-related pricing structures and coverage nuances. What works for your neighbor's parents might not be optimal for yours.
