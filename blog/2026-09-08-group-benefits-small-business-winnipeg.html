What Group Benefits Coverage Can Include

Group benefits in Manitoba typically include several components. Understanding what's available helps you choose what's right for your business:

Health Insurance

This covers medical expenses like prescription drugs, vision care, and hearing aids. Many plans also include coverage for chiropractors, physiotherapists, and other paramedical professionals.

Dental Insurance

Dental plans help cover routine checkups, cleanings, fillings, and even more extensive procedures. Prevention is always more affordable than emergency treatment.

Life Insurance

Group life insurance provides financial protection for your employees' families. This is often offered at a lower rate than individual policies because the risk is spread across the group.

Disability Insurance

Short-term and long-term disability coverage helps employees maintain income if they're unable to work due to illness or injury.

Mental Health and Wellness Benefits

Many modern plans include counseling services, meditation apps, and mental health support—increasingly important in today's workplace.

Affordable Group Benefits Options for Small Businesses in Winnipeg

The good news? You don't need to be a large corporation to access group benefits. In Manitoba, there are several options designed specifically for small business owners:

Traditional Group Plans

These work best if you have 10 or more employees. Insurers pool the risk across your group, which keeps costs manageable.

Voluntary Benefits

Employees pay part or all of the premium for coverage they select. This lets you offer benefits without bearing the entire cost.

Guaranteed Issue Plans

These require minimal underwriting, making them perfect for smaller groups where employees have varying health situations.

Customizable Coverage

You can choose what to include and what to skip. Start with the essentials—maybe health and dental—and add more as your business grows.
