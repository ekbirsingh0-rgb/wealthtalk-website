What Is Super Visa Insurance and Why Do You Need It?

The Super Visa is a temporary resident visa specifically designed for parents and grandparents of Canadian citizens or permanent residents. It allows them to stay in Canada for up to two years on a single entry—significantly longer than a standard visitor visa.

Here's the critical part: Super Visa insurance is mandatory. Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) requires proof of private health insurance before they'll approve your parent's or grandparent's Super Visa application. This isn't optional or something you can skip.

Why? Because Super Visa holders cannot access provincial health care coverage during their first three months in Canada. Super Visa insurance bridges this gap and protects both your family and the Canadian health care system from unexpected medical costs.

Understanding Super Visa Insurance Requirements in Calgary

When you're applying for Super Visa insurance in Calgary or anywhere else in Alberta, there are specific requirements set by IRCC that your policy must meet:

  • Minimum coverage: At least $100,000 CAD in emergency medical coverage
  • Duration: Coverage must be valid for the entire duration of the Super Visa (up to 24 months)
  • Coverage start date: Insurance must start on or before the date your parent or grandparent arrives in Canada
  • Age limits: While there's no maximum age, premiums increase significantly for applicants over 60 or 65
  • Pre-existing conditions: Most policies require declaration of existing health conditions
  • Deductible: IRCC typically accepts policies with deductibles up to $5,000

Living in Calgary specifically doesn't change these requirements, though Alberta's health care system specifics are important to understand when choosing coverage.

How Much Does Super Visa Insurance Cost in Calgary?

The cost of Super Visa insurance varies based on several factors. For a typical parent in their mid-60s applying for one year of coverage, you can expect to pay between $500–$1,500 CAD. For two-year coverage, costs typically range from $800–$2,500 CAD.

Factors that affect your premium include:

  • Age of the applicant (the biggest factor)
  • Duration of coverage (1 year vs. 2 years)
  • Pre-existing medical conditions
  • Overall health status
  • The insurance provider you choose

Tips for Getting the Best Super Visa Insurance in Calgary

Start early: Don't wait until the last minute. Apply for insurance at least 4–6 weeks before your parent's expected arrival in Canada.

Be transparent about health: Always declare pre-existing conditions accurately. Failing to disclose health information can result in claim denials later.

Compare providers: Different insurance companies offer different rates and coverage options. What's cheapest isn't always best—you want reliable coverage that meets IRCC requirements.

Keep documentation: Save your insurance certificate and policy details. You'll need these for the Super Visa application and to show Canadian customs when your parents arrive.

Consider renewal: If your parents are staying beyond the initial coverage period, plan ahead for renewal to avoid coverage gaps.
