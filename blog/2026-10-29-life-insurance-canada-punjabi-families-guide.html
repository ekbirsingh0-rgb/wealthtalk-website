Why Life Insurance Matters for South Asian Families in Canada

In South Asian culture, family support and responsibility run deep. You're not just thinking about your spouse and children — you may also be supporting aging parents, cousins, or helping siblings back home. This broader circle of responsibility makes life insurance even more critical for Punjabi families.

According to recent data, the average Canadian household needs between $300,000 to $500,000 in life insurance coverage, though South Asian families often require more due to extended family obligations and potential sponsorship responsibilities.

Without proper life insurance coverage, your family could face:

  • Loss of income that pays for daily living expenses
  • Inability to pay off the mortgage or car loans
  • Disruption to children's education plans
  • Reduced ability to support aging parents or relatives
  • Unexpected debt burden on surviving family members

That's why finding the right life insurance Canada plan designed for your family's unique needs isn't optional — it's essential.

Types of Life Insurance Available to Punjabi Canadians

When you start looking at life insurance options, you'll encounter two main types: term life insurance and permanent life insurance.

Term Life Insurance

Term life insurance is the most popular choice for families. You pay a fixed premium for a set period — typically 10, 20, or 30 years — and if something happens to you during that term, your beneficiaries receive the death benefit. It's straightforward, affordable, and perfect if your main goal is protecting your family during their most dependent years.

For example, a healthy 35-year-old Punjabi Canadian can get a $500,000 20-year term policy for as low as $30-50 per month. That's incredibly affordable protection.

Permanent Life Insurance

Permanent insurance (whole life or universal life) provides lifelong coverage and includes a savings or investment component. It's more expensive but offers benefits like cash value accumulation and the ability to leave a legacy. Many South Asian families use this for estate planning and wealth transfer to the next generation.

How Much Life Insurance Do Punjabi Families Really Need?

This depends on your specific situation. As a general rule, experts recommend having 7-10 times your annual income in coverage. But for South Asian families with extended support obligations, you may need more.

Consider these factors when calculating your needs:

  • Your annual salary and household expenses
  • Outstanding debts (mortgage, car loans, credit cards)
  • Children's education costs (college or university)
  • Support for aging parents or relatives
  • Any financial obligations to family members abroad
  • Spouse's income and ability to manage alone

At WealthTalk with Ekbir, we help families work through this calculation. Every family is different, and your coverage should reflect your unique situation.

Getting Life Insurance as a Punjabi Family in Canada

The application process for life insurance Canada is straightforward. You'll need to provide health information, undergo a medical exam (for larger amounts), and declare any existing conditions or lifestyle factors.

Some good news: Canadian insurers have become much better at understanding health conditions common in South Asian populations, including diabetes, high blood pressure, and heart disease. If you have any of these, you can still get coverage — it may just cost a bit more.

If you've been declined elsewhere or have health concerns, don't worry. There are options, including guaranteed issue policies, though these typically come with higher premiums.

Many families put off getting life insurance because they worry about cost or medical exams. But here's the truth: it's more affordable than you think, and the protection you gain is invaluable. The best time to apply is when you're healthy — premiums are lower, and you're more likely to qualify.
