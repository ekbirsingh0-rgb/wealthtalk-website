What Is Super Visa Insurance and Why Is It Required?

Super Visa insurance is a private health insurance policy specifically designed for parents and grandparents visiting Canada. Unlike standard visitor insurance, it's a mandatory requirement from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) when you sponsor a family member under the Super Visa program.

The primary purpose is to protect Canadian taxpayers by ensuring that visiting seniors have coverage for healthcare needs during their stay. This includes doctor visits, hospital stays, prescription medications, and emergency treatments. Without this insurance, your aging parents could face significant out-of-pocket medical expenses, and your application could be refused outright.

Think of it as peace of mind for both you and your parents. Medical emergencies can happen unexpectedly, and having proper coverage means your family won't face financial hardship if your mother needs hospitalization or your father requires emergency surgery.

Current IRCC Super Visa Insurance Requirements for 2025

Here's what IRCC specifically requires for Super Visa insurance in 2025:

  • Minimum Coverage Amount: The policy must provide a minimum of $200,000 in emergency medical coverage. This covers hospital stays, emergency dental work, and urgent medical procedures.
  • Coverage Period: The insurance must be valid for the entire duration of the Super Visa — typically one year from the date of arrival, though it can be extended.
  • Pre-Existing Conditions: The policy must include coverage for pre-existing medical conditions. This is crucial for older visitors who may have diabetes, heart conditions, or arthritis.
  • Canadian Insurance Company: The insurance must be issued by a licensed Canadian insurance provider or underwriter authorized by provincial insurance regulators.
  • Letter of Acceptance: You must provide an official letter from the insurance company confirming coverage meets IRCC requirements at the time of application.

Medical Exams and Health Requirements

Many families ask whether their parents need a medical exam to get Super Visa insurance. Here's the good news: most Super Visa insurance policies do not require a formal medical examination. However, you will need to provide health information when applying.

The insurance company will ask questions about:

  • Current medical conditions and diagnoses
  • Current medications and dosages
  • Recent hospitalizations or surgeries
  • Age and general health status

This information helps insurers assess risk and determine premiums. It's important to be honest and complete when filling out health questionnaires — omitting information could result in claim denial later.

If your parents have significant health issues, some insurers may request a medical report from their doctor back home. This actually strengthens your Super Visa application by showing transparency and thorough preparation.

Key Coverage Details and Exclusions

Super Visa insurance covers emergency medical services, but it's important to understand what's included and what isn't:

Typically Covered:

  • Hospital accommodation and procedures
  • Doctor consultations and diagnostics
  • Prescription medications
  • Emergency dental treatment (up to limits)
  • Ambulance services
  • Medical evacuation if needed

Typically NOT Covered:

  • Routine or elective procedures
  • Ongoing treatment for chronic conditions started before arrival
  • Vision and hearing aids
  • Dental work beyond emergencies
  • Travel delays or baggage claims

Understanding these distinctions helps you prepare financially for your parents' visit and avoid surprises at the healthcare provider's office.
