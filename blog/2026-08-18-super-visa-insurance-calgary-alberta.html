What is Super Visa Insurance and Why Do You Need It in Calgary?

Super Visa insurance is a specialized health insurance policy required by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) for parents and grandparents visiting Canada on a Super Visa. Think of it as a safety net that protects both you and your family members.

Here's why it matters: When your parents arrive in Canada, they won't be covered by Alberta Health Services for the first three months of their stay. If they need medical attention during this period—whether it's a hospital visit, emergency room care, or prescription medications—those costs can be astronomical. Super Visa insurance covers these expenses so you don't face unexpected bills of thousands of dollars.

The insurance also demonstrates to IRCC that you're financially prepared to support your parents' visit, which strengthens your application significantly.

Super Visa Insurance Requirements in Calgary: What IRCC Demands

IRCC has specific requirements for Super Visa insurance that all Calgary residents must follow:

  • Minimum Coverage: Your policy must cover at least $100,000 CAD in medical expenses
  • Duration: Coverage must be valid for at least one year from the date your parents arrive in Canada
  • Pre-existing Conditions: The policy must cover pre-existing medical conditions with no waiting period
  • Canadian Insurer: The insurance must be provided by a licensed Canadian insurance company
  • Deductible: Maximum deductible of $10,000 CAD per claim

These aren't suggestions—IRCC absolutely requires these terms. If your insurance doesn't meet these specifications, your Super Visa application will likely be rejected. How Much Does Super Visa Insurance Cost in Calgary?

The cost of Super Visa insurance varies based on several factors specific to your situation:

  • Age of Your Parents: Older parents typically cost more to insure. A parent aged 60 might pay $700-$1,200 annually, while a parent aged 75+ could pay $2,000-$4,000+
  • Health History: Pre-existing conditions like diabetes, high blood pressure, or heart disease affect premiums
  • Coverage Duration: One-year policies are standard, but some families need multi-year coverage
  • Number of Visitors: Covering both parents costs more than covering one

In Calgary specifically, you'll find competitive rates from Canadian insurers. The good news? Super Visa insurance is remarkably affordable compared to the medical costs it protects against. A $1,500 annual policy that prevents a $50,000 hospital bill is an excellent investment.
