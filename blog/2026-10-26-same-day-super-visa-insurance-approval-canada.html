What Is Super Visa Insurance and Why Do You Need It?

Super Visa insurance is a specialized medical coverage plan designed specifically for parents and grandparents visiting Canada. Unlike regular travel insurance, Super Visa insurance meets the strict requirements set by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).

Here's what makes it essential:

  • It's a mandatory requirement for Super Visa applications
  • It must provide a minimum of $200,000 in emergency medical coverage
  • Coverage must last for the entire duration of your loved one's stay in Canada
  • It protects your family from catastrophic medical bills if your parent or grandparent requires hospitalization

Without proper Super Visa insurance approval, your family member's visa application could be delayed or rejected. That's why getting same-day approval is such a game-changer for families in Winnipeg and across Canada.
