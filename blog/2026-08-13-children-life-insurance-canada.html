Children's Life Insurance Canada: Should You Get It?

As a parent in Canada, you want to protect your family's financial future. One question that comes up often is whether children's life insurance makes sense. It might seem counterintuitive—after all, you insure things that provide income or have significant financial value. But children's life insurance isn't always about replacing income. Let's explore what it is, why families consider it, and whether it's right for you.

Understanding Children's Life Insurance in Canada

Children's life insurance is a policy that pays out a lump sum death benefit if your child passes away. In Canada, these policies are typically available for children from birth (or a few weeks old) through age 25 or older, depending on the insurer.

There are two main types:

  • Term Life Insurance: Covers your child for a specific period (10, 20, or 30 years). It's affordable but expires after the term ends.
  • Permanent Life Insurance: Covers your child for their entire lifetime and builds cash value over time. It's more expensive but never expires.

Unlike adult life insurance, which focuses on replacing lost income, children's policies address different financial realities. They help cover funeral and burial costs, outstanding debts, and provide emotional and practical support during an unimaginable time.

When Should You Consider Children's Life Insurance?

The answer depends on your family's situation. Here are scenarios where it makes sense:

Funeral and Final Expenses

In Canada, funeral costs typically range from $3,000 to $7,000 or more. For South Asian Canadian families, traditional ceremonies and gatherings can add significant expenses. A children's policy with a $10,000 to $25,000 benefit can cover these costs without burdening your family during grief.

Existing Debts

If your child has accumulated debt (perhaps through education or personal loans), life insurance proceeds can help settle these obligations, ensuring they don't fall on surviving family members.

Building Cash Value Early

If you choose permanent insurance, locking in rates when your child is young means lower premiums over their lifetime. As they grow, they can take over the policy and maintain coverage into adulthood with very affordable rates.

Income-Earning Children

Older teenagers who work and contribute to family expenses may benefit from coverage that replaces their financial contribution.

The Cost Factor: Is It Affordable?

Children's life insurance is surprisingly affordable in Canada. Here's what you might expect:

  • Term life insurance for a child: $8–$20 per month for $25,000 in coverage
  • Permanent life insurance: $25–$50 per month for the same benefit

These premiums are fixed when you apply and don't increase as your child ages (for permanent policies). Because children are generally healthy with minimal health risks, insurers charge very low rates.

For many Canadian families, especially those building comprehensive financial protection, the cost is manageable. However, budget matters. If money is tight, prioritizing life insurance for income-earning parents comes first.

Why Parents Choose Children's Life Insurance

Beyond practical reasons, many families value the peace of mind. In the South Asian Canadian community, there's often a cultural emphasis on protecting children and planning for all possibilities. Life insurance reflects these values while providing concrete financial protection.

Additionally, permanent policies offer a gift: your child can maintain coverage throughout their life at the rate locked in during childhood. This is valuable if they later develop health conditions that might make insurance expensive or unavailable.

What About Your Family?

The decision is personal. Consider these questions:

  • Could your family afford unexpected funeral and final expenses?
  • Do you want to provide your child with affordable, locked-in coverage for life?
  • Is there peace of mind value in knowing this protection exists?
  • Can you comfortably fit the monthly premium into your budget?

If you answered yes to most of these, children's life insurance might be right for your family.
