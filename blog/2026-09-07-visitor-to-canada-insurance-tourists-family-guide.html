What is Visitor to Canada Insurance and Why Do You Need It?

Visitor to Canada insurance is a temporary health insurance policy that covers medical emergencies for people visiting Canada from abroad. Unlike Canadian residents who have provincial health coverage, tourists and visiting family members don't have access to our publicly funded healthcare system.

Here's what many families don't realize: if your visiting parent or family member gets sick or injured in Canada, medical costs can be extremely expensive. An emergency hospital visit can cost thousands of dollars. A helicopter evacuation could run $15,000 to $50,000. Without insurance, these bills fall directly on your family.

That's why visitor to Canada insurance exists—to protect your guests and your finances from unexpected medical emergencies.

What Does Visitor to Canada Insurance Cover?

Good visitor insurance policies typically include:

  • Emergency hospital and physician visits – doctor appointments and emergency room care
  • Prescription medications – necessary medicines prescribed during the visit
  • Emergency dental work – for pain relief only (not routine cleaning)
  • Ambulance services – ground and air ambulance coverage
  • Medical evacuation – if your loved one needs to be transported home for treatment
  • Trip interruption coverage – if illness forces them to cut their visit short
  • Pre-existing condition coverage – optional add-on (important for older parents with health conditions)

Who Needs Visitor to Canada Insurance?

If someone is visiting Canada and they're not a Canadian resident or citizen, they need visitor insurance. This includes:

  • Parents visiting on a Super Visa
  • Grandparents coming to meet new grandchildren
  • Relatives attending weddings or family celebrations
  • Friends taking a vacation in Canada
  • International students (before their provincial coverage begins)

Many South Asian families I work with bring their parents to Canada for extended stays. If your parent is over 65 or has existing health conditions, securing the right coverage becomes even more critical.

Types of Visitor to Canada Insurance Plans

Single-Trip Insurance: Best for one visit. Usually covers 1 to 364 days and costs $2-5 per day depending on age and coverage level.

Annual Multi-Trip Insurance: Perfect if your family visits multiple times per year. One policy covers all trips within 12 months. Often more economical than buying single policies.

Super Visa Insurance: Specifically designed for parents and grandparents visiting Canada on a Super Visa. Covers the entire length of stay (typically 2 years) and includes higher coverage limits.

Group/Family Plans: If multiple family members are visiting together, group rates can save money.

How Much Does Visitor to Canada Insurance Cost?

Costs depend on several factors:

  • Age: Older visitors pay more (rates jump significantly at 65 and 75)
  • Duration: Longer stays cost more, but per-day rates drop
  • Coverage level: Higher limits cost more
  • Pre-existing conditions: Adding coverage for existing health issues increases premiums

How to Apply for Visitor to Canada Insurance

The application process is straightforward:

  • Gather your visitor's basic information (name, date of birth, passport number)
  • Decide on coverage duration and type
  • Disclose any pre-existing medical conditions
  • Choose your coverage level
  • Receive instant or next-day approval
  • Get your policy documents by email

Most policies start coverage the very day your visitor arrives in Canada. Some allow backdating if your guest is already here.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Don't wait until your visitor is already in Canada—apply before they arrive. Don't skip coverage for pre-existing conditions if applicable. Don't assume lower-cost plans have adequate coverage. Always read what's actually covered.
