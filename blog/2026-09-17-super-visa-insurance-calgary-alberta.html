What Is Super Visa Insurance and Why Do You Need It?

Super Visa insurance is mandatory health insurance specifically designed for parents and grandparents visiting Canada under the Super Visa program. Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) requires this insurance as a condition of approval.

Here's the simple truth: without valid Super Visa insurance, your parents' application will be rejected. It's that important.

The insurance covers:

  • Emergency medical care while visiting Canada
  • Hospital stays and procedures
  • Doctor visits and emergency dental work
  • Prescription medications for acute conditions
  • Medical evacuation if necessary

Many families underestimate how expensive medical emergencies can be in Canada. A single hospitalization can easily cost $10,000 to $50,000 or more. Without proper insurance, you could face devastating financial consequences while your parents struggle with health issues far from home.

Super Visa Insurance Requirements in Calgary

If you're sponsoring your parents to visit Calgary or anywhere else in Canada, the IRCC has specific insurance requirements you must meet:

  • Minimum coverage: $100,000 CAD for emergency medical care
  • Insurance must be valid for the entire duration of your parents' stay in Canada (up to 24 months)
  • The policy must be purchased before submitting the Super Visa application
  • Proof of insurance must be included with the application package
  • The insurance provider must be licensed to sell insurance in Canada
  • Your parents may have medical requirements or pre-existing condition assessments

One thing many families don't realize: Super Visa insurance is different from travel insurance. Travel insurance typically covers shorter trips and excludes pre-existing conditions. Super Visa insurance is specifically designed for longer visits and may cover certain pre-existing conditions—which is crucial for elderly parents.

Super Visa Insurance Costs and What Affects Your Premium

The cost of Super Visa insurance in Calgary depends on several factors:

  • Age of your parents: Premiums increase significantly with age. A 60-year-old will pay less than a 75-year-old.
  • Length of stay: Longer visits cost more. A 24-month policy costs more than a 12-month policy.
  • Pre-existing medical conditions: If your parents have diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, or other conditions, this affects pricing.
  • Health status: Some insurers require medical underwriting.
  • Insurance provider: Different companies have different rates and coverage options.

On average, you might expect to pay anywhere from $500 to $2,500+ per parent for a 12-month policy, depending on age and health status. For two parents on a two-year policy, you're looking at $2,000 to $5,000+.

Yes, this is an investment. But it's far less expensive than a medical emergency without coverage.
