What Are Group Benefits and Why Do Small Businesses Need Them?

Group benefits are insurance plans offered by employers to their employees, typically covering health care, dental, vision, disability, and life insurance. Unlike individual plans, group benefits spread the risk across many people, which means lower premiums for everyone involved.

For small businesses in Winnipeg, group benefits offer several advantages:

  • Employee Retention: Employees with good benefits are significantly more likely to stay with your company. This means less turnover and lower hiring costs.
  • Tax Deductions: Premiums paid by employers are generally tax-deductible business expenses, reducing your overall tax burden.
  • Competitive Edge: In today's job market, offering benefits makes your small business competitive against larger employers.
  • Employee Wellness: When employees have access to preventive care, dental, and vision coverage, they're healthier and more productive.
  • Simplified Administration: Group plans handle billing and claims centrally, reducing administrative work for you.

According to Statistics Canada, businesses offering group benefits report higher employee satisfaction and lower absenteeism rates—directly impacting your bottom line.

Types of Group Benefits Available in Manitoba

When exploring group benefits for small business in Winnipeg, you'll typically encounter these coverage options:

Health Care Coverage

This includes prescription drugs, paramedical services (physiotherapy, chiropractic), and other medical expenses not covered by provincial health plans. Most plans cover 80-100% of eligible expenses after deductibles.

Dental Insurance

Covers preventive care (cleanings, exams), basic procedures (fillings, extractions), and major work (crowns, bridges). Plans typically cover 100% preventive, 80% basic, and 50% major expenses.

Vision Coverage

Provides benefits for eye exams and helps with the cost of glasses or contact lenses. Many plans cover $150-$300 for eyewear every two years.

Disability Insurance

Both short-term and long-term disability protect employees (and your business) if someone becomes unable to work due to illness or injury. This is especially valuable for key employees.

Life Insurance

Typically offered as a multiple of salary—for example, 1x, 1.5x, or 2x annual earnings. This provides financial security for employees' families and demonstrates your commitment to their wellbeing.

Common Questions About Group Benefits in Winnipeg

Do I need a minimum number of employees?
Most group plans require at least 2-5 employees, though some carriers work with solo operators. Requirements vary by carrier.

Can I customize coverage?
Absolutely. Group plans are flexible—you can choose coverage levels, deductibles, and which benefits to include based on your budget and priorities.

What if an employee leaves?
Employees typically have the option to convert to individual coverage or continue under group coverage until they secure new employment benefits.

How do costs work?
Costs are shared between employer and employee, though you determine the split. Many Winnipeg businesses cover 50-100% of premiums to stay competitive.
