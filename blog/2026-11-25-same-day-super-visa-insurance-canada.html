What is Super Visa Insurance and Why Do You Need It?

The Super Visa is a special immigration category that allows Canadian citizens and permanent residents to invite their parents and grandparents to visit Canada for extended periods—up to two years. It's a wonderful program for families who want quality time together without the stress of frequent short visits.

However, there's a mandatory requirement: your parents must have private health insurance coverage. This isn't optional—Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) requires proof of insurance before approving a Super Visa application.

Super Visa insurance typically covers:

  • Emergency medical expenses (up to $200,000 CAD or more)
  • Hospital stays and emergency treatments
  • Prescription medications during emergencies
  • Medical evacuation if needed

The insurance must cover the entire duration of the Super Visa stay and be from a Canadian insurance provider approved by IRCC.
